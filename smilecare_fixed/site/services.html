KNOW MORE ABOUT OUR TREATMENTS

Detailed Dental Services

Learn about our treatment options, common benefits and consultation process. Your dentist will recommend care based on your individual dental condition.

SERVICE 01 / PREVENTIVE CARE

General Dentistry

General dentistry focuses on maintaining healthy teeth and gums through routine examinations, preventive care and treatment of common dental problems. Regular visits can help identify concerns at an early stage.

What This Service Includes

  • Dental checkups
  • Oral examinations
  • Professional cleaning
  • Cavity assessment
  • Gum health evaluation
  • Preventive guidance
Consultation: Your dentist will examine your teeth and gums and explain any recommended care.
Book Consultation
SERVICE 02 / COSMETIC DENTISTRY

Professional Teeth Whitening

Teeth whitening is a cosmetic dental treatment that may help improve the appearance of stained or discoloured teeth. A dental professional can assess the cause of discolouration and discuss suitable whitening options.

Service Highlights

  • Smile assessment
  • Stain evaluation
  • Whitening options
  • Shade consultation
  • Aftercare guidance
  • Oral health review
Important: Whitening suitability varies by patient. Your dentist should assess tooth and gum health before treatment.
Book Consultation
SERVICE 03 / RESTORATIVE DENTISTRY

Dental Implants

Dental implants are one option for replacing missing teeth. They involve a dental implant placed in the jawbone to support a replacement tooth. Treatment suitability depends on bone health, gum condition and overall dental assessment.

Implant Service Includes

  • Initial consultation
  • Oral examination
  • Treatment planning
  • Implant assessment
  • Restoration discussion
  • Follow-up planning
Consultation: Your dentist will discuss whether implants or another tooth-replacement option may be appropriate for you.
Book Consultation
SERVICE 04 / TEETH ALIGNMENT

Orthodontics & Braces

Orthodontic treatment focuses on correcting the position of teeth and improving the way the upper and lower teeth meet. Treatment options may include traditional braces or other suitable alignment methods.

Orthodontic Care Includes

  • Teeth alignment check
  • Bite assessment
  • Braces consultation
  • Treatment planning
  • Progress monitoring
  • Retainer guidance
Consultation: An orthodontic assessment helps determine the appropriate treatment approach and expected follow-up schedule.
Book Consultation
SERVICE 05 / ENDODONTIC CARE

Root Canal Treatment

Root canal treatment may be recommended when the inner part of a tooth becomes infected or damaged. The procedure involves treating the affected tissue and restoring the tooth as advised by the dentist.

Treatment Information

  • Tooth examination
  • Pain assessment
  • Diagnostic evaluation
  • Treatment discussion
  • Tooth restoration plan
  • Follow-up guidance
Important: Tooth pain or swelling should be assessed by a dental professional. Treatment depends on the condition of the tooth.
Book Consultation
SERVICE 06 / PEDIATRIC DENTISTRY

Children's Dentistry

Children's dentistry focuses on protecting young smiles through age-appropriate dental examinations, preventive care and oral hygiene education. A friendly approach can help children become comfortable with dental visits.

Children's Dental Care Includes

  • Children's checkups
  • Tooth development review
  • Cavity assessment
  • Oral hygiene guidance
  • Preventive care advice
  • Parent consultation
For parents: Regular dental visits and age-appropriate oral hygiene habits can support children's dental health as they grow.
